# Author social logos

These marks come from the platforms' official brand downloads. Preserve their
geometry, proportions, and approved colors when updating them.

- **GitHub:** `github-black.svg` and `github-white.svg` are the unmodified
  Invertocat SVGs from the [GitHub brand kit](https://brand.github.com/foundations/logo)
  ([archive](https://brand.github.com/GitHub_Logos.zip)).
- **LinkedIn:** `linkedin-blue.png` and `linkedin-white.png` are the unmodified
  `LI-In-Bug.png` and `InBug-White.png` from the
  [LinkedIn downloads](https://brand.linkedin.com/downloads)
  ([archive](https://content.linkedin.com/content/dam/me/business/en-us/amp/xbu/linkedin-revised-brand-guidelines/logos/in-logo.zip)).
- **X:** `x-white.svg` is the original `logo.svg` from the
  [X brand kit](https://about.x.com/en/who-we-are/brand-toolkit)
  ([archive](https://about.x.com/content/dam/about-twitter/x/brand-toolkit/x-logo.zip)).
  `x-black.svg` uses the same geometry with the approved black fill.

The website link uses Lucide's generic globe icon, since it has no platform brand.
